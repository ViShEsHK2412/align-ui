Slider — the hard cases

Every section states what to do and what should happen. If what happens is different, that is a bug and the page has already named it. Nothing here is styled by the page: the control brings its own look from the tool's tokens, so what you see is what it looks like in the panel.

16 · Two hundred of them

Scroll the list fast, then drag one while scrolling. Watch the frame time at the bottom of the page.

Only the one being dragged should be doing any work. Nothing animates at rest, so the worst frame while scrolling should stay near 16ms. Reset the worst-frame counter before each attempt.

17 · Reduced motion

Turn on your system's reduce-motion setting and reload, then click a slider rather than dragging it.

The click jumps instead of springing, and dragging past the end no longer stretches the track. Both are decoration: the value has already changed either way. Dragging and keyboard stepping were never animated, so they look identical.
